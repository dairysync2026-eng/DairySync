01

Start with a secure sign-in

Use the account and role assigned to you by your administrator.

FIRST STEPS
  1. Open the DairySync web app and enter your registered email address or username.
  2. Enter your password and choose the sign-in button.
  3. Confirm that your name and role appear in the app header, then begin at the Command Center.
ⓘForgot your password?Enter your registered email address in the sign-in form and use the password-reset option. Follow the link sent to your email.
02

Find your way around

The app opens on the Command Center and shows navigation for your authorized work areas.

Navigation at a glance

  • Command Center: role-specific summary, alerts, and shortcuts.
  • Subsystem tabs: open ingredients, production, cold storage, sales, and other permitted areas.
  • Header: check your account and role, notifications, profile, and online/offline indicator.
  • Tab strip on small screens: swipe, drag, scroll, or use the arrow controls to reveal more tabs.

Use the dashboard as a shortcut

Start from your Command Center to review the metrics and alerts relevant to your role. Choose a dashboard card or action to open its related subsystem. When a tab is not available to your account, ask an administrator rather than trying another role.

🔒Access is tied to your account.Tabs and actions vary by role. A restricted page does not mean your data is lost; it means your account is not authorized for that area.

04

Administer staff accounts and app branding

System Administrators can manage operational staff access and the shared DairySync logo without accessing business inventory.

SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION · STAFF ACCOUNTS
  1. Open System Administration and review the account totals and connection status.
  2. To add a staff member, enter their name, email, username, job title, department, and operational role, then choose Create account & send setup email.
  3. Find an account in the directory to edit its staff profile, change an allowed operational role, send a password-reset email, or disable/reactivate access.
  4. Refresh the directory to confirm that the requested change has been applied.
SYSTEM BRANDING · SHARED APP LOGO
  1. Use Change logo to select a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image while online.
  2. Confirm that the updated logo appears in the application header and browser tab.
  3. Choose Restore default to return to the standard DairySync logo.

Changing the shared logo does not update icons already installed on a device’s home screen.

🔒Respect elevated-role safeguards.System Administrators can manage operational roles but cannot grant or modify Developer or System Administrator access. They cannot change their own account role or disable their own account. Ask the Lead Developer to provision privileged accounts. Administrative changes are recorded for oversight.
05

Manage ingredients and physical counts

Keep material records current so teams can plan purchases and production against usable stock.

RAW INGREDIENTS · EVERYDAY TASKS
  1. Open Raw Ingredients; search or filter by category, low stock, or expiry.
  2. Open a material record to review stock, unit, supplier, expiry, reorder point, and safety stock.
  3. Use the available add, edit, or stock-adjustment action for an authorized update. Record the correct quantity and unit.
  4. Check the updated stock and any reorder or expiry warning. Export or print a report when needed.
PMO INVENTORY · PERIOD RECONCILIATION
  1. Open PMO Inventory and create a draft period for Raw Ingredients or Finished Goods.
  2. Add the existing items to count and record physical ending quantities and any required movement details.
  3. Compare the calculated expected balance with the physical count; resolve differences before posting.
  4. Post the reviewed period once. Posted periods are locked to avoid a second reconciliation.
⚠Count once; avoid duplicate entries.For live-ledger periods, purchases, production, sales, and other recorded movements are derived from shared transactions. Do not enter the same movement again as a manual adjustment. Posted reconciliation changes shared stock.
06

Set up a recipe and run production

Production consumes recipe materials when a batch is created and transfers its output to Cold Storage when completed.

BEFORE THE FIRST BATCH
  • Confirm the finished product exists in Cold Storage.
  • Confirm its recipe/BOM is configured and required ingredients exist in Raw Ingredients.
  • Check material availability and verify the recipe's quantities and units.
  • If the product has no recipe, ask a Developer, Plant Manager, or other authorized recipe manager to configure it first.
BATCH JOURNEY
Scheduled→Pasteurization / Cooking→Cooling→Bottling & Packaging→Finished Goods

Choose the product, target quantity, raw milk volume, assigned staff, and notes when creating a batch. Review the selected recipe and deductions, then advance the batch through each recorded production step.

01 · Prepare🧾Check recipe & stockVerify product, ingredient availability, and units.
02 · Create🧑‍🍳Record a batchSet target, milk volume, assigned staff, and notes.
03 · Process🌡️Advance each stepUpdate the batch as work progresses on the floor.
04 · Release❄️Complete & verifyConfirm output appears in Cold Storage.
✓Cancel carefully.A scheduled batch may be cancelled; its deducted recipe ingredients are restored. Check the batch status and stock records after cancellation.
07

Monitor cold storage and request stock

Cold Storage is the shared record for finished products available to the operation, retail, and commitments.

Cold Storage check

  • Search or filter products by category and stock condition.
  • Review current quantity, location, capacity, safety stock, and allocations.
  • Investigate low-stock or overstock warnings; use a stock adjustment only when authorized and supported by a physical check.
  • Print a cold-storage audit sheet or export the records when required.

Dairy Box replenishment request

  1. Store Outlet staff submit a finished-goods request from the dashboard.
  2. Track the request status and cancel it if it is still pending and no longer needed.
  3. The Plant Manager reviews the request and approves or rejects it.
  4. For an approved request, allocate available stock to fulfill it; inventory changes when the request is fulfilled.
ⓘA request is not a stock movement.Submitting a request does not reserve or deduct stock. Allocation occurs when the Plant Manager fulfills the approved request.
08

Plan replenishment and commitments

Use reorder information to guide purchasing, and commitments to track planned supply against demand.

ROP PROCUREMENT
  1. Open ROP Procurement and review items below their reorder point or safety stock.
  2. Check supplier, lead time, maximum stock, packaging needs, and recent receipts.
  3. Record a supplier arrival or receipt after confirming what physically arrived. Include waybill or notes when available.
  4. Verify the updated ingredient stock and transaction history.
SUPPLY & DEMAND
  1. Create a commitment with its partner, item, target quantity, unit, and scheduled date.
  2. Set its type, priority, and status based on the agreed plan.
  3. Update fulfilled quantity and status as supply is delivered.
  4. Review upcoming or delayed commitments; include a reason when cancelling.

Reorder alerts are based on configured stock thresholds; they are decision support, not an automatically placed purchase order.

10

Review alerts, reports, and audit history

Use these tools to monitor activity and keep an operational record.

Notifications

Open the header notification area to review unread alerts. Alerts may highlight low stock, reorder points, overstock, and other operational conditions. Mark reviewed alerts as read.

Reports & exports

Use available report actions to preview and print inventory records or download Excel/CSV data. Audit reports may include filters, a summary, notes, and sign-off information.

Audit Trail

Search and filter recorded actions by category, severity, or date. Open a record for details. Access and controls depend on your role.

ⓘKeep records factual.Use notes for clear, concise context such as a receipt reference or the reason for a reconciliation. Audit history supports review; follow your site's recordkeeping policy.
11

Work safely when connectivity changes

DairySync can show cached data and queue supported changes when offline; not every workflow is an offline transaction.

When you see the offline indicator

  • Check the connection indicator in the app header.
  • Cached data may be older than the shared cloud records.
  • Some supported updates can remain queued until the connection returns.
  • After reconnecting, allow synchronization to finish and verify the updated record before repeating an action.

Reliable stock habits

  • Do not repeat a sale, receipt, or batch because the screen appears slow; first check whether it completed.
  • Coordinate simultaneous stock deductions for the same product or ingredient.
  • Check current stock before confirming a physical adjustment or count.
  • Report a failed sync or unexpected balance to your supervisor or administrator.

Quick answers

A few common questions to help you keep work moving.

I cannot see a tab I need. What should I do?

Confirm you signed in to the correct account. Tabs are role-based. Contact your system administrator if you believe your account needs different access.

A batch cannot be created for a product. Why?

Check that the product has a configured recipe/BOM and that its required raw ingredients are available. Ask an authorized recipe manager to configure a missing recipe.

Why is a sale blocked?

The cart may request more units than available stock. Adjust the quantity, remove an unavailable item, or ask the responsible stock custodian to verify inventory.

Did my offline change reach the shared records?

Check the connection status and allow queued supported changes to synchronize. Verify the record after reconnecting; do not submit the same stock movement again unless you have confirmed the first attempt failed.

How can I keep a copy of this manual?

Choose Print / Save PDF at the top of the page, then select your browser's print or save-as-PDF destination.
